# A Tides Near Me alternative that works with no signal

Tides Near Me does one thing very well: open it, see the nearest station, know whether the
water is rising or falling. That is why it has 163K ratings. People go looking for an
alternative for two reasons.

- **Ads.** The free tier shows Google AdMob ads. Premium, a subscription listed at $14.99
  or $1.99, removes them and adds 30-day forecasts.
- **No offline claim.** Its listing never says it works without signal, and at the point,
  in the islands, in the anchorage, signal is often what you do not have.

If you run a boat, currents are the other difference. Since version 4.0 in October, a Tides
Near Me current station shows the last and next current with speed, the next slack, and the
day's curve. It gives the slack as a time, not a window with a duration, and the listing
makes no offline claim for it.

## Slackwater, in short

Free, no ads, no account, no tracking. Tides worldwide and currents across the US and
Canada. Bundled stations predict on your phone without a connection. Canadian stations need an initial download; some passes require a connection and are labeled in the app. For a current station it gives the next slack,
how long it lasts, and max flood and ebb with direction.

It is available on the App Store and needs iOS 26. Its standalone Apple Watch app is free. Alerts, station calendars, lock-screen widgets and Watch complications are optional Premium features, with annual and lifetime purchase options.

Slackwater features checked October 9, 2026; Tides Near Me's details checked against its US App Store listing and website on October 14, 2026.

## The details that matter

**Rising or falling, instantly.** This is the thing Tides Near Me nailed, so it is the
thing Slackwater's list had to match. Stations sort nearest first. Each card draws a day
of its curve with a marker at now, so you read the state from the shape before you read
the number. Nothing to tap.

**Offline predictions.** Slackwater bundles harmonic data for thousands of stations and computes their predictions on the phone for any date. Most Canadian stations build an offline model from CHS predictions after an initial download. Some Canadian passes remain online-only and are labeled in the app. Map areas you have already viewed are cached for use without a connection.

**Currents with an answer.** Deception Pass ebbing at 3.1 kn, next slack in 5h 26m, and it
lasts 19 minutes. That last number decides whether you go, and it is the one most tide
apps do not have.

**Where the numbers come from.** Every station names its source and its datum. The engine
is checked against NOAA's and CHS's own published predictions, and the deviations are
written down on slackwater.xyz.

## Who should switch

Switch if you use the app on the water without signal, want to know how long slack lasts,
or are done with ads.

Stay with Tides Near Me if you prefer its simple next-tide view, want marine weather beside
the tide, check from a beach with signal and the ads do not bother you. It still answers that
question well.

## Moving over

Nothing to import. Open Slackwater, allow location if you like (it stays on the device),
and your nearest stations are already there. Search for the rest by name and star them.
Two minutes.

Predictions are not observations. Weather, river flow and local conditions change the
water. Slackwater is not for navigation.
