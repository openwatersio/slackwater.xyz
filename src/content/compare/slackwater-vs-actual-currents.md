# Slackwater vs Actual Currents

**Short version.** Actual Currents shows you the water moving: animated particles over a
nautical chart, with a time scrubber, a wind overlay and route analysis. It is the best
picture of flow on a phone. Its model data loads over a connection, and the free tier is
three hours either side of now. Slackwater's bundled stations predict on the phone without a connection. Canadian stations need setup, and some passes remain online-only. Its tide and current predictions are free. Both cover Canada, and both now show tides.

They are different tools and plenty of people will want both.

Slackwater features checked October 9, 2026; Actual Currents' details checked against its US App Store listing and website on October 14, 2026.

## What each one answers

Actual Currents answers "what is the whole strait doing at 16:30?" Drag the scrubber and
watch Rosario Strait turn. If you are planning a route across a body of water and want to
see the set along the way, nothing else on iPhone does this. Its Optimal Departure tool
times a route at every departure in a window, and saved places show the next slack at a
point you pick.

Slackwater answers "can I get through this pass, and when?" Pick the station, and the
readout is the state now, the next slack and how long it lasts, max flood and ebb with
speed and set, over a curve you scrub through the week, with the reference port's tide on
the same screen. Both apps show tides: Actual Currents through a virtual tide gauge and
NOAA tide stations, Slackwater through tide stations worldwide.

## Offline

Actual Currents loads its model data over a connection and lists no offline mode. Slackwater computes bundled predictions on the phone for any date. Most Canadian stations build an offline model after a download; some Canadian passes require a connection and are labeled in the app.

## Free tier

Actual Currents' free tier is three hours either side of now. Planning tomorrow's departure
is $4.99 a month or $39.99 a year. Slackwater's predictions are free for any date. Optional Premium adds alerts, station calendars, lock-screen widgets and Watch complications, with annual and lifetime purchase options.

## What Actual Currents has that Slackwater does not

The flow picture, the wind overlay, route planning with current analysis and departure
timing, trip logging, and the global ocean surface currents. Slackwater is a station app and does not draw a flow field.

## Who should pick which

**Actual Currents** if you want to see the flow across a whole area, plan a route against
it, and you do that at the dock with signal.

**Slackwater** if you want the slack window at a station, offline, free, with tides.

**Both** if you plan on Actual Currents and run on Slackwater.

Predictions are not observations. Weather, river flow and local conditions change the
water. Neither app is for navigation.
