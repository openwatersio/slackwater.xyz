# Slackwater pricing

Slackwater is free to download. Tide charts, tidal current predictions, station search, favorites, home screen widgets and the Apple Watch app are free. Every prediction date is available without a subscription. There is no Open Waters account and no ads.

## Free

Use the free app to check high and low tides, current speed and direction, and slack-water times. Browse the map or search for stations, save favorites, and scrub the timeline to a chosen date. Bundled stations work offline. Supported Canadian stations need an initial download; some passes require a connection and are labeled in the app.

## Premium yearly — US$7.99 per year

Premium supports Slackwater's development and adds lock-screen widgets, Apple Watch complications, alerts before the tides and currents you care about, and station calendars.

The yearly subscription renews automatically until you cancel. Manage or cancel it in your Apple account's subscription settings. The purchase screen in the app shows the price and renewal terms before you confirm.

## Premium lifetime — US$69.99 once

A one-time lifetime purchase unlocks the same Premium features without a recurring subscription. Both purchase options are available inside Slackwater. If you already own Premium, use Restore purchase in the app with the Apple account that bought it.

## Prices and availability

These US App Store prices were checked on October 15, 2026 against [Slackwater's listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/slackwater-tides-current/id6813654837). Apple shows your local currency and the current price for your storefront before purchase. Prices and availability can vary by region and may change; the purchase screen is the final source for what you will pay.

Slackwater requires iOS or iPadOS 26 or later, or watchOS 26 or later on Apple Watch. Coverage varies by place; [browse the station directory](/stations/) before a trip. Predictions are not observations, and Slackwater is not for navigation.
