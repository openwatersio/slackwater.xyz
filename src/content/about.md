# About Slackwater

Slackwater is a tide and tidal currents app built by [Open Waters](https://openwaters.io/), published by Open Water Software LLC. It helps people check high and low tides, current speed and direction, and slack-water times when planning time on the water or at its edge.

The app runs on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. Tide stations are available around the world; current stations cover the US and Canada. Coverage varies by place. The [station directory](/stations/) lets you browse the stations published on this website. The website and app do not have identical coverage.

Tide charts, current predictions, station search, favorites and home screen widgets are free, with no account or ads. Optional Premium adds alerts, station calendars, lock-screen widgets and Watch complications. Bundled stations work offline. Supported Canadian stations need initial setup, and some passes require a connection.

Slackwater's [app source code](https://github.com/openwatersio/slackwater-ios) is public under the GPL. The [accuracy report](/accuracy/) explains published prediction comparisons and their limits. Predictions are not observations: weather and river flow affect real conditions. Slackwater is not for navigation.

If Slackwater has helped you, [rate it on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6813654837?action=write-review), share it with a friend, or [buy Premium](/pricing/#payment), which funds its development. [Ways to help](/support/#help) lists them in order.

[Download Slackwater](https://apps.apple.com/app/slackwater-tides-current/id6813654837), [contact us](/contact/) for help, or read the [privacy policy](/privacy/) for how the app and website handle data.
