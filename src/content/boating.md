# Anchored, no bars

Your phone roamed, the data stopped, and every tide app on it is a spinner. Slackwater is a free tide and current app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch that predicts on the phone itself, so the week of tides and currents ahead is already there in the anchorage, mid-crossing, or with the boat's electronics down.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/slackwater-tides-current/id6813654837)

## Time the pass from the anchorage

Open a pass and Slackwater shows the current right now, its speed and direction, the time to the next slack, and a week of maximum floods and ebbs underneath. Drag the timeline to any hour and the whole screen follows.

Slack windows work for boats too. In Settings, set Comfort current to the fastest current your boat is happy to carry through a pass. Every current curve then marks the stretches under that speed in green, and dragging the timeline stops at each window's opening and closing, so you can plan the morning's departure from the night before.

## Before you leave the dock

Bundled stations predict for any date with nothing to load: tides around the world and currents across the United States. Canadian stations download once from the Canadian Hydrographic Service while you have a connection, and then work offline. A few Canadian passes need a connection every time, and the app labels them. Set up the stations on your route before you lose signal.

Check that your cruising ground is covered in the [station directory](/stations/).

## The week ahead, nearest first

One list holds both kinds: a harbour's height and a pass's speed and set, each with the next thing it does and how far away it is. Where you are sits on top, favourites under it, the rest by distance.

![The station list located at Friday Harbor: the harbour's tide on top, the nearest current under it, a saved favourite, and nearby stations below, each with its curve for the day.](/shots/list.webp)

## At the helm

The Apple Watch app works without your phone: turn the Digital Crown to move through the day. A free home screen widget shows the next high, low or slack and today's curve. With Premium, lock-screen widgets and Watch complications put the next turn where you glance, and an alert can tell you when a slack window opens.

![Friday Harbor on Apple Watch, turned with the Digital Crown to 3:49pm: 6.1 feet and rising toward a 6.9 foot high at 5:31pm.](/shots/watch/watch-tide.webp)

## Charts first

Predictions are not observations. Wind, pressure and river outflow change what the water does, and a prediction cannot see them. Keep your charts, tide tables and current atlas aboard, and use Slackwater to plan around them. Slackwater is not for navigation.

## Free, with no account

Every station and every date is free, with no account and no ads, and the app is [open source](https://github.com/openwatersio/slackwater-ios). Optional [Premium](/pricing/) adds alerts, station calendars, lock-screen widgets and Watch complications.

The passes on the BC coast each have a guide: [Active Pass](/currents/active-pass/), [Dodd Narrows](/currents/dodd-narrows/), [Gillard Passage](/currents/gillard-passage/), [Sechelt Rapids](/currents/sechelt-rapids/) and [Seymour Narrows](/currents/seymour-narrows/).
